1The problem

Every complaint starts a clock

The car maker wants the suspect parts stopped within a day and the root cause within days. The records the engineer needs sit in different systems: production lots, machine logs, inspection results and shipments.

  1. Day 0Complaint and photo arrive
  2. 24 hoursD3: contain the suspect parts
  3. 5 daysD4: name the root cause
  4. 20 daysD5: corrective actions in place
The quick answer: "operator error"Easy to write. The real cause stays in the machine and the complaint comes back.
The safe-looking answer: hold everythingHundreds of good lots stopped, and the customer's line can stop with them.

Deadlines from the MAHLE supplier 8D manual. A stopped car line costs about $2.3 million an hour (Siemens, 2024). Car makers take about seven weeks to confirm a root cause (McKinsey, 2026).

2How it works

From the customer's email to a signed 8D

  1. Complaintemail, photo, date codesAny language. Paste it or drop a file.
  2. IntakeGeminiTranslates the email and boxes the defect next to a good part.
  3. InvestigatorADK agent and BigQuery MLTests press, cavity, shift, crew and resin lot, one tool at a time.
  4. Challenger10 rules in codeSends back blame without proof, made-up numbers and the wrong machine.
  5. WriterGeminiWrites the 8D in English and Japanese, each claim linked to evidence.
  6. Engineera personSigns D2 to D5. Nothing leaves before that.

The Challenger sends a weak draft back to the Investigator, up to three times.

Each stage keeps its colour on every page: red when the complaint arrives, blue when the 8D is signed.

How to upload your own complaint

  1. Paste the email or drop a file. A .txt or .eml file works, in any language. The form reads the date codes and the defect from the text.
  2. Add a photo if you have one. JPEG, PNG or WebP, up to 8 MB. Gemini boxes the defect and you confirm it.
  3. Check the defect type and the date codes. They must fall between 30 Aug and 16 Oct 2026, the demo plant's history.
  4. Press Start. The agents work for one to two minutes. The draft opens in the workbench, and only you can see it.

8What next

For a plant that wants to try it

  1. One plant, one part family, four weeks. Read-only access to the five tables.
  2. Replay the last closed 8Ds and compare the drafts with what the team concluded.
  3. Run new complaints alongside the current process before anyone relies on it.

Known limits: the plant data here is synthetic, photo checks are qualitative, and the Challenger checks evidence rather than engineering judgement, which is why a person signs.
