              How it works
              
  
              When a car maker finds bad parts, the supplier owes it an 8D report. The customer sets the clock: stop the bad parts within a day, find the root cause within about five, fix it within about twenty. Under that pressure people write "operator error" and hold all the stock to be safe. This tool drafts the 8D from the plant's own records, shows the evidence for every claim, and refuses a cause the data doesn't support. A quality engineer reviews it and signs.
