                How it works
                
  
                A customer's complaint goes in. An 8D report with evidence behind every line comes out, and a person signs it.
                

  
                  
    
                1. Intake, on GeminiReads the email and the photoAny language. Boxes the defect next to a good part.
                  2. 
    
                2. Investigator, on Gemini through ADKTests every suspect in BigQuery
      is_is_notstratifyparam_excursionsinspection_changescontainmentpast_8d
      BigQuery ML contribution analysis finds where the defects concentrate.
                  3. 
    
                3. Challenger, rules in codeSends weak drafts backNo evidence, a made-up number, a missing escape cause or blame without proof: back to the Investigator, up to three rounds.
                  4. 
    
                4. Writer, on GeminiWrites the 8D in English and JapaneseEvery number is checked against the evidence.
                  5. 
    
                5. Quality engineerApproves D2 to D5, then exportsExport stays locked until a named person signs.
